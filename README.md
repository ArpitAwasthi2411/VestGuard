# VestGuard

### A smart vest that detects falls and calls for help — built for elders living alone in the Himalayan hills

![Patent](https://img.shields.io/badge/patent-pending%20(IN%20202611122870)-17375E)
![Award](https://img.shields.io/badge/State%20Level%20Smart%20Hackathon%202026-1st%20Prize-E39A2D)

</div>

## Why

About **one in three** older adults falls every year. The fall is often not the worst part — the **wait** is. An elder who lives alone may lie on the floor for hours before anyone finds them. In hill villages, where houses are far apart and children work in the cities, that wait is longer.

## The vest

<div align="center"><img src="images/fig_vest.png" width="85%" alt="VestGuard vest, front and back"/></div>

VestGuard is a familiar Himachali-style vest with the electronics hidden inside. The elder simply wears it — there is no button to press. When a fall happens, the vest raises a loud local alarm and alerts the family on their phones.

## Results

295 real-time trials with the V2 prototype:

<div align="center"><img src="images/results.png" width="80%" alt="Results"/></div>

| Test set | Trials | Correct | Rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Falls detected (12 fall types) | 150 | 142 | **94.7%** |
| Daily activities correctly ignored (10 activities) | 145 | 140 | **96.6%** |
| **Overall** | **295** | **282** | **95.6%** |

## Testing

All trials were done by young volunteers, with falls only onto safety mats.
